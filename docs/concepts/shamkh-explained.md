# Shamkh Explained

## What is Shamkh?

**Shamkh (شامخ)** is Iran's official Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI), published monthly by the **Iran Chamber of Commerce, Industries, Mines and Agriculture (ICCIMA)**.

## History

- **Launched**: 2019
- **Publisher**: ICCIMA (ایکاما)
- **Frequency**: Monthly
- **Format**: PDF reports in Persian

## How Shamkh is Calculated

Shamkh is a weighted average of 11 sub-indicators:

### Core Indicators (70%)

| Indicator | Persian | Weight |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Production | تولید | 20% |
| New Orders | سفارشات جدید | 20% |
| Sales | فروش | 15% |
| Raw Materials Inventory | موجودی مواد اولیه | 5% |
| Final Goods Inventory | موجودی محصول نهایی | 5% |
| Input Price | قیمت مواد اولیه | 5% |
| Production Expectations | انتظارات تولید | 5% |

### Extended Indicators (30%)

| Indicator | Persian | Weight |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Employment | استخدام | 10% |
| Exports | صادرات | 10% |
| Delivery Speed | سرعت تحویل | 5% |
| Business Activity | فعالیت اقتصادی | 5% |

## Interpreting Shamkh

| Shamkh Value | Economic Condition |
|--------------|-------------------|
| > 55 | Strong Expansion |
| 50 - 55 | Moderate Expansion |
| 45 - 50 | Mild Contraction |
| < 45 | Severe Contraction |

## Data Sources

- **PDF Reports**: Available at [iccima.ir](https://iccima.ir)
- **Archive**: Historical reports on [otaghiranonline.ir](https://otaghiranonline.ir/tag/11491)

## Limitations

1. **Survey-based**: Subject to respondent bias
2. **Manufacturing-focused**: Primarily covers industrial sector
3. **Lagging release**: Reports published 1-2 months after reference period
4. **Language barrier**: Reports in Persian only
